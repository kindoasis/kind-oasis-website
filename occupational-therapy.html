Occupational therapy helps when pain, injury, illness, disability, cognitive change, stress, or a life transition makes everyday activities harder. We begin with what you need and want to do, then build care around your goals and your real environment.

What mobile OT can address

  • Hand and upper-extremity rehabilitation: arthritis, fractures, nerve conditions, wounds, post-operative recovery, custom orthoses, and persistent hand or arm pain.
  • Daily activities and independence: dressing, bathing, meal preparation, household tasks, caregiving, work, hobbies, and community participation.
  • Neurologic and cognitive changes: practical strategies for movement, coordination, memory, attention, planning, and daily routines.
  • Aging well at home: home safety, adaptive equipment, fall-risk reduction during daily tasks, caregiver education, and sustainable routines.
  • Grief, stress, and life transitions: rebuilding structure, meaningful activity, coping strategies, and emotional regulation.
  • Therapeutic movement and nature-based activity: yoga, breathwork, walking, hiking, or other meaningful movement when appropriate for your goals.
Why care in context matters: Seeing the actual chair, staircase, kitchen, tool, hobby, or routine often reveals opportunities that a clinic cannot. Recommendations can be tested where you will really use them.

What a visit may include

Your first visit includes your story, priorities, and a focused evaluation. Depending on your needs, treatment may include task practice, therapeutic exercise, manual techniques, education, adaptive strategies, custom orthosis fabrication, caregiver training, or changes to routines and surroundings.

Telehealth may be appropriate when coaching, education, movement practice, or environmental problem-solving can be completed safely by video. We will help determine the right setting for your care.

Serving the Fredericksburg area
