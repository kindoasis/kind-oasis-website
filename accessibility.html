Our commitment

Accessibility statement

Last updated September 12, 2026

Kind Oasis Integrative Health wants our website and services to be welcoming and usable for people of all abilities. We work to support readable text, keyboard navigation, meaningful headings, descriptive image text, and sufficient color contrast.

Need assistance?

If any part of this website is difficult to use, or if you need information in another format, please contact us. We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or assistance you need.

Email info@kindoasisintegrativehealth.com or call/text (540) 227-4448.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As the website changes, we will continue reviewing content and functionality for barriers.
