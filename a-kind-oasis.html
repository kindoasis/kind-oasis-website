A sample for right now

Let your eyes wander.

Find a comfortable spot, indoors or outside. A window view, a houseplant, or a nature image can be your starting point.

Open your invitation
  1. Let your gaze move around without searching for anything special.
  2. Notice one color, one shape, and something moving. If there is no movement, notice something still.
  3. Choose whichever detail interests you. Look at its edges, texture, or the way light falls across it.
  4. Before you finish, look around your whole space again. Leave when you are ready.

A smaller version: Notice one color and one shape. That can be the whole practice.

From a seat or window: Choose details visible from your seat. Keep your head in a comfortable position.

A few helpful details

Make it yours.

Do I have to go outdoors?

No. You can use these invitations at home, from a seat, beside a window, or outdoors. A houseplant or nature image can be a starting point.

What format is the guide?

An eight-page illustrated PDF, suitable for reading on a device or printing. It is a self-guided collection, without appointments or a subscription.

Is this occupational therapy?

This is a general wellness and reflection resource. Purchasing or using it does not establish a therapy relationship and does not include individualized assessment or treatment.

Can I buy it now?

The guide is coming soon. In the meantime, you’re welcome to try the sample invitation above.
