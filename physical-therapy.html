Mobile physical therapy

Physical therapy built around how you move every day.

Individualized PT in your home or community for strength, balance, mobility, pain, and recovery—without the distractions of a busy clinic.

Physical therapy helps people improve movement and function when pain, weakness, injury, surgery, balance changes, or a health condition gets in the way. Mobile care lets treatment connect directly to the stairs, surfaces, routines, and activities you navigate every day.

What mobile PT can address

  • Balance and fall-risk reduction: walking, transfers, lower-body strength, confidence, and strategies for moving more safely.
  • Joint-replacement and orthopedic recovery: individualized progression after surgery or injury, guided by your medical team’s precautions.
  • Neck and back pain: movement assessment, education, exercise, and practical changes that support daily function.
  • Strength and mobility: rebuilding capacity for stairs, walking, getting up from a chair, and returning to valued activities.
  • Musculoskeletal concerns: focused care for movement limitations, discomfort, and loss of function.
  • Home exercise that fits: a realistic plan shaped around your space, equipment, schedule, and goals.
Why PT at home? Treatment can use the exact environment you need to navigate. That may mean practicing your own stairs, finding a workable chair-height strategy, or building a walking plan that fits your neighborhood.

What a visit may include

The evaluation considers your movement, strength, balance, symptoms, health history, environment, and goals. Treatment may include individualized exercise, movement practice, manual therapy, education, pacing, and a home plan designed to be useful between visits.

Your plan is adjusted over time based on response and progress. When coordination with a surgeon, physician, or another clinician is needed, we work within the instructions and precautions established for your care.
