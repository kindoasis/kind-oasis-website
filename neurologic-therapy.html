Neurologic rehabilitation · Fredericksburg, VA

Support for movement and daily life after neurologic change.

We bring one-on-one OT and PT into the routines and spaces where progress needs to matter.

Call or text (540) 227-4448Email Kind Oasis →

Conditions such as Parkinson’s disease or stroke can affect movement, coordination, attention, fatigue, and everyday activities in different ways. We begin with your goals and assess which therapy approach is appropriate.

Care shaped around your needs

  • OT: self-care, hand use, routines, cognitive strategies, home setup, equipment, and caregiver education.
  • PT: walking, transfers, strength, balance, and movement practice.
  • Working together: coordinated goals when both OT and PT services are appropriate.

Why the real setting helps

Practicing a transfer at your own chair or working through a routine in your kitchen can reveal barriers and useful strategies. Your care plan is adjusted to your condition, safety needs, support system, and medical guidance.
